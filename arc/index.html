WHAT ARC DOES

Everything You Need to Run Email Campaigns.
Nothing You Don't.

Your Own Sending Account
ARC sends through your connected Outlook or Office 365 account. Your emails come from you — not a third-party server — so they land in inboxes, not spam folders.
Image-Based Campaigns
Design your campaign in Canva or any design tool, export it as an image, and upload it to ARC. No HTML templates, no coding, no complexity.
Contact Management
Add contacts manually or import a CSV. ARC deduplicates automatically so the same person never receives your campaign twice.
Real-Time Delivery Stats
See exactly who received your campaign and who didn't — per contact, in real time as emails go out.
Echo Integration
ARC unlocks automatically after 15 enrichments in Echo. Import your enriched contacts directly with no CSV needed.
Multi-Account Sending
Connect up to 3 Outlook accounts and choose which account sends each campaign — perfect for teams managing multiple brands.
