THE OBJECTION SYSTEM

First Objection Is Never the Real One.
CLOISE Gives You Three Loops to Find Out.

THE REALITY
"Not Interested" Is a Reflex. Not a Decision.
Most cold callers hear the first pushback and fold. The truth is that 80% of first objections are reflexes — the prospect hasn't even processed what you said yet. The reps who close know this. They have a system for what comes next.
  • Most first objections are reflexes — not real decisions
  • Reps who wing it lose. Reps with a system close
  • Without structure, you either cave or push too hard and lose rapport
  • Training reps to handle objections properly takes months — CLOISE does it instantly
THE CLOISE METHOD
3 Loops. 10 Rebuttals. One Universal Close.
Every AI-generated script in CLOISE includes 10 custom rebuttals — each built on the exact objections real people give in your industry. Each rebuttal has three escalating loops before the universal close.
  • Loop 1: Clarify the real objection, deflect, counter, bridge back into script
  • Loop 2: Acknowledge, pivot to benefits, soft close attempt
  • Loop 3: Final counter, final bridge, final close attempt
  • Universal close fires after all three loops — no call ends without a proper attempt
