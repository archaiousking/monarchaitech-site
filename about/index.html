THE THOUGHT BEHIND ECHO

Echo Was Built Because Finding Canadian Business Data Should Not Be This Slow.

Echo was created by Shah Noor Babar, a Canadian entrepreneur with hands-on experience in sales, lead generation, business development, and building customer pipelines from the ground up.

The idea did not come from theory. It came from doing the work.

Finding companies. Searching for contacts. Looking up business owners. Checking websites. Moving between tabs. Copying information into spreadsheets. Trying to turn scattered details into something useful.

Anyone who has done real B2B prospecting knows the pain. The work is slow, repetitive, and frustrating.

Echo was built because that process should be faster.

Small and Mid-Sized Businesses Need Better Access to Company Data.

Large enterprise teams often have access to expensive business intelligence platforms. Smaller Canadian businesses usually do not.

They are left with Google searches, directories, scattered websites, manual spreadsheets, and outdated lists.

Sales teams lose time before they even start outreach. Recruiters spend hours researching employers. Consultants build lists manually. Business owners rely too heavily on referrals and word of mouth.

Monarch Technologies was built to give Canadian professionals a better starting point.

OUR MISSION

Make Canadian Business Intelligence Accessible to Every Serious B2B Professional.

Business intelligence should not only belong to companies with massive data budgets.

A local service company should be able to find businesses in its target market. A recruiter should be able to research Canadian employers. A consultant should be able to build clean company lists.

That is the mission behind Monarch Technologies.
