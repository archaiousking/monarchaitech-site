SIGNING OPTIONS

Single Signer or Multi-Signer.
Your Document, Your Rules.

SINGLE SIGNER
Fast, Simple, Done.
Upload your document and send one secure link to one signer. They sign, you get notified, the document is complete.
  • Perfect for NDAs, proposals, and simple agreements
  • Signer receives a secure unique link via email
  • Signed document and audit certificate generated instantly
  • You are notified the moment they sign
MULTI-SIGNER
Sequential or Simultaneous.
Send to multiple signers in a defined order, or to all signers at once. Each signer gets their own secure link.
  • Perfect for contracts, partnership agreements, and onboarding
  • Set signing order — next signer is notified automatically
  • Full audit trail shows every signer's timestamp
  • Document marked complete only when all parties have signed
